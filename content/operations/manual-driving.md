## Symptom

Daily procedure — not a fault. Use this when an AGV needs to be driven manually, for example to reposition it, move it out of a blocked area, or for testing.

## Access Level

🟢 **Operator – Level 1**

## Step-by-Step Procedure

1. **Switch the AGV to Manual mode** (see [Auto / Manual Mode](auto-manual-mode.md)).
2. **Check the surrounding area** is clear before moving.
3. **Use the handheld pendant / joystick** to drive the AGV slowly and deliberately.
4. **Keep line of sight** with the AGV at all times while driving manually.
5. **Avoid driving through safety-sensitive zones** (blind corners, doorways, other traffic) faster than walking pace.
6. **Stop and park** the AGV in a safe, designated location once done.
7. **Switch back to Auto mode** when manual driving is complete, if the AGV should resume automatic tasks.

## Expected Result

AGV is repositioned safely with no collisions or near-misses, and correctly returns to Auto mode when manual driving is finished.

## If Not Solved

If the AGV does not respond to manual controls, treat this as a fault — see [AGV Stopped / Not Moving](../troubleshooting/agv-stopped.md).

## Escalation

Contact **VisionNav Service** if manual controls are unresponsive or behave unexpectedly.

## Information Required

- AGV ID
- Location where manual driving was needed
- Time of occurrence
