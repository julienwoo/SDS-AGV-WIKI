# AGV stopped / not moving

**Access level:** Operator · Level 1

## Symptom
The AGV has stopped and cannot continue the current task.

## Step 1 — Check the AGV status
Check the HMI / AGV status screen for:
- Alarm
- Emergency Stop
- Manual Mode
- Safety Laser Alarm
- Low battery

## Step 2 — Check the safety area
Confirm that there are no people, pallets or other obstacles in the AGV safety area.

## Step 3 — Check the alarm
If an alarm is displayed, open the corresponding troubleshooting article in this Wiki.

## Step 4 — Check the operating mode
Confirm that the AGV is in **Auto Mode** when automatic operation is required.

## Step 5 — Resume the task
After the abnormal condition has been cleared, follow the project procedure to reset or resume the task.

## If the problem is not solved
Contact **VisionNav Service**.

Provide:
- AGV ID
- Alarm code / message
- RCS Task ID
- Current location
- HMI screenshot
- Approximate time of occurrence

> **Safety:** Never bypass safety functions or modify safety parameters to recover an AGV.
