# RCS communication problem

**Access level:** Supervisor · Level 2

## Symptom
The AGV appears offline, cannot receive tasks, or communication with RCS is abnormal.

## Step 1 — Check RCS status
Confirm that RCS is running normally.

## Step 2 — Check AGV connection status
Confirm whether the affected AGV is shown as Online / Connected.

## Step 3 — Check for multiple affected AGVs
If several AGVs are offline simultaneously, escalate as a possible system or network issue.

## Step 4 — Do not change network configuration
Do not modify IP addresses, firewall rules, DNS or other network settings unless authorized.

## Escalation
Provide the AGV ID, RCS status, alarm message, time of occurrence and screenshots.
