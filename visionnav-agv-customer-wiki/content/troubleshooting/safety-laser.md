# Safety laser alarm

**Access level:** Operator · Level 1

## Symptom
The AGV stops because the safety laser has detected an object or an abnormal condition.

## Step 1 — Check the safety area
Confirm that no person, pallet, vehicle or other obstacle is inside the protected area.

## Step 2 — Remove a normal obstacle
If a normal obstacle caused the stop, remove it only when it is safe to do so.

## Step 3 — Check the laser status
Confirm that the safety laser alarm has cleared.

## Step 4 — Reset the AGV
Follow the project-approved reset procedure.

## Step 5 — Confirm Ready
Confirm that the AGV returns to a normal Ready / Auto state.

## If the alarm remains
Do **not** change safety parameters or bypass the safety function.

Contact VisionNav Service and provide the AGV ID, alarm information and a screenshot.
