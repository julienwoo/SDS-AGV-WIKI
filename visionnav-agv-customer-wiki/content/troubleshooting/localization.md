# Localization problem

**Access level:** Supervisor · Level 2

## Symptom
The AGV cannot localize correctly, reports a localization error, or cannot continue navigation.

## Step 1 — Stop new tasks
Avoid repeatedly sending new tasks until the situation is understood.

## Step 2 — Check the environment
Check whether pallets, equipment, vehicles or other objects have changed significantly compared with the normal operating environment.

## Step 3 — Confirm the AGV position
Confirm the actual physical position of the AGV and compare it with the RCS / navigation view.

## Step 4 — Follow the project recovery procedure
Use only the recovery procedure approved for this project.

## Escalation
Do not modify maps or navigation parameters unless instructed by VisionNav Service.
