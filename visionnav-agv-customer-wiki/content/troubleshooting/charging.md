# Charging problem

**Access level:** Operator · Level 1

## Symptom
The AGV cannot start charging, charging is interrupted, or the battery does not increase as expected.

## Step 1 — Check the charging station
Confirm that the charging station is powered and has no active alarm.

## Step 2 — Check the charging area
Confirm that the AGV can safely reach the charging position.

## Step 3 — Check AGV alignment
Confirm that the AGV is correctly positioned for charging.

## Step 4 — Check battery status
Check the battery SOC and any charging-related alarm.

## Step 5 — Retry charging
After the abnormal condition is cleared, follow the project procedure to retry charging.

## Escalation
Contact VisionNav Service with the AGV ID, battery status, charger status and screenshots.
