# AGV Start-up

**Access level:** Operator · Level 1

## Standard procedure
1. Confirm that the operating area is safe.
2. Check the AGV for visible damage or abnormal conditions.
3. Turn on the main power according to the project SOP.
4. Wait for the system to complete start-up.
5. Check the HMI for alarms.
6. Confirm that the AGV reaches Ready status.
7. Select Auto Mode when automatic operation is required.
8. Confirm the AGV is Online in RCS.

> Replace these steps with project-specific photos and exact switch locations before customer release.
