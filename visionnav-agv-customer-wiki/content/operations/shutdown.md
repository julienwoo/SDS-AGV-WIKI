# AGV Shut-down

**Access level:** Operator · Level 1

## Standard procedure
1. Complete or safely stop the current task.
2. Move the AGV to the project-defined safe position.
3. Confirm that no mechanical action is in progress.
4. Exit Auto Mode if required by the project SOP.
5. Shut down the AGV according to the approved procedure.

> Do not use an emergency stop as a normal shut-down procedure.
